# TimetableSlot

> **Kind:** Molecule
> Token-only styling. No raw hex / px / font-family literals.


Single class period block on the timetable screen.

Layout: time on left, subject + teacher in the middle, room badge on the right. Heights: 64px on mobile, 56px on web.

Prop `status`: `upcoming | live | done` — `live` adds a Sangat Red pulsing dot.
