# SiteFooter

> **Kind:** Organism
> Token-only styling. No raw hex / px / font-family literals.


4 columns at `lg`+, 2 at `md`, 1 at `sm`.
1. Logo + tagline + address
2. Quick links (`web.footer.quickLinks`)
3. Contact (phone, email, hours, map link)
4. Social (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook icons)

Bottom strip: `web.footer.rights` · privacy · terms.
Background: `color.deepIndigo`. Text: `color.text.onBrand` / `color.neutral.300` for secondary.
