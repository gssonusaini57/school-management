## Principal letter
`principal.jpg` — Square, head-and-shoulders, soft natural light, neutral background. Warm but professional. ≥ 800×800px.

## Voices
`voice-{1..6}.jpg` — Square portraits of 6 community voices: 2 parents, 2 students, 1 teacher, 1 alumna. Same crop, same warm tone.

## Programs
One representative photo per program (Foundation Play, Primary, Middle, Secondary, Sr. Sec — 3 streams). Classroom in action.

## Gallery
60–80 photos across three years (2024, 2025, 2026), sorted into events: Founder's Day, Independence Day, Annual Sports Day, Cultural Day, Science Fair.

## Naming convention
`<page>-<slot>-<NN>.jpg`. Lowercase, dash-separated. Provide both 1×, 2× and a WebP at < 200 KB for web; PNG fallback for letterhead.

## Consent
All photographs of identifiable students require signed parent consent on file (form: `/admin/forms/photo-consent.pdf`). Do not publish unconsented imagery.
